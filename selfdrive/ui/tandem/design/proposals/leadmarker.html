Lead marker: other ideas

Shapes other than the arrow, each over the blue path from option A. Left: following. Right: braking for the car ahead.
1 · Ground barRECOMMENDED
  • A rounded bar on the road just under the car ahead, as if the path ends against it.
  • Reads as "this is the car I'm following" without an arrow pointing at it.
  • White while following; amber while braking for it (right), with the Slowing for traffic chip.
2 · Path stops at the car
  • No separate marker: the path runs up to the car ahead and stops, capped with a bright edge.
  • The gap you're keeping is the empty road between the cap and the car; it shrinks as you close in.
  • Without a lead, the path runs to the horizon as today. The cap turns amber while braking (right).
3 · Focus frame
  • Four corner ticks around the car ahead, like a camera locking focus.
  • Sized to the car, so it grows as you close in; nothing sits on the road.
  • White while following, amber while braking for it (right). Lead Insights' distance tag sits above the frame.
