Recommended: blue path and ground bar, in each situation

Option A's path with marker 1, a thin dark outline for bright roads, opacity for confidence, a short settle-in, and amber tied to the braking chip.
Following
  • A white bar on the road under the car ahead, with a thin dark outline.
Braking for the car ahead
  • The bar turns amber at the same moment as the Slowing for traffic chip: one rule, braking harder than 0.3 m/s².
Unsure there's a car
  • When the model isn't sure the car ahead is real, the bar fades to half opacity instead of changing color.
A new car ahead
  • Someone cuts in: the bar grows in over a quarter second (left to right), then holds still.
Stopped behind a car
  • At a light the bar stays white and calm; the Stopped chip already says why you're waiting.
Cars in the next lanes (Lead Insights)
  • Adjacent cars get the same bar at low opacity, with no outline, so the car you're following stays the obvious one.
  • Lead Insights' distance tag sits above the car ahead.
Bright sun
  • Without the outline (left) a white bar washes out on a pale road; the thin dark outline (right) keeps it readable.
No car ahead
  • No marker: just the blue path to the horizon.
