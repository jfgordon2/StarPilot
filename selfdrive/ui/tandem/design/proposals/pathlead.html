Path and lead marker: proposals

The path shows where openpilot is steering; the marker shows the car it's following. Today both take StarPilot's colors: the path from its color scheme, the marker's glow always yellow.
TodayCURRENT
  • Path: StarPilot's color scheme or its Path Color setting (here yellow). Standard doesn't choose one.
  • Marker: a red arrow (the theme's lead color) with a hard yellow glow, drawn by upstream.
  • Neither color says anything an Essentials color doesn't, and yellow reads as a warning.
A · Essentials blueRECOMMENDED
  • Path: our blue, strongest at the car, fading toward the horizon, as the mocks draw it.
  • Marker: a white arrow with a soft blue glow, so it reads as part of the path.
  • Amber and red stay free for warnings: the steering arc, Help steer and alerts.
B · Quiet white
  • Path: a soft white wash, the least color on the road.
  • Marker: a white arrow ringed in dark, like the radar dots, so it holds up over bright roads.
  • The calmest option; the path recedes behind the chips and bar.
C · Blue, marker shows braking
  • Path: Essentials blue, as in A.
  • Marker: white while following (left), amber while the car brakes for it (right), the same moment the Slowing for traffic chip shows.
  • Says one more thing, at the cost of one more color on the road.
