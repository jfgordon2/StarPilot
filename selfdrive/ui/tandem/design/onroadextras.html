Onroad extras: StarPilot's optional visuals, in Essentials

Each shows only when its StarPilot switch is on. Same information as before, in our colors, icons and type.
LEAD INSIGHTS · PEDALS
38
mph
  • Two tiles under the driver monitor tile, top right: brake, then gas.
  • Lit in red (brake) or green (gas) while the car brakes or accelerates; dim at 35% otherwise.
  • Only with PedalsOnUI on (Lead Insights).
EXTERNAL GPU · MODEL SOURCE
38
mph
Big model
  • One status chip under the driver monitor tile, as the status chips are styled.
  • Big model (green), Loading model (blue), Big model failed (amber), Small model in use (amber).
  • Shows only with an external GPU, and fades 2.5 s after the state settles, as StarPilot's does.
COMPASS
62
mph · NE
  • The heading joins the unit line under the speed: mph · NE.
  • No separate text; it uses the speed card's secondary color.
  • Only with StarPilot's compass on.
STOPPING POINT
24
mph
45 m
  • A white line across the path where the car plans to stop, replacing StarPilot's red STOP octagon.
  • A small tag above it gives the distance, in JetBrains Mono like Developer View.
  • Only with Show Stopping Point on; the distance only with its metrics on.
LANES · BLIND SPOT
  • Adjacent lanes get a faint blue wash; a lane with a car in the blind spot turns amber.
  • Path edges are a thin light-blue line along the path.
  • Rainbow path stays StarPilot's own, a deliberate fun option.
RADAR POINTS · CURVE GLOW
  • Radar points: white dots ringed in black, 10 px, no labels.
  • Curve glow (curve speed control acting): a soft blue inner glow at the edges, under the HUD, alongside the Slowing for curve chip.
BORDER · SIGNALS AND BLIND SPOT
54
mph
  • Turn signal: that half of the border turns blue (#5AAAF8), pulsing with the signal.
  • Blind spot: that half turns red (#E5646F), held while the car is there.
  • These cover the engaged green; alerts and Help steer still take the whole border.
